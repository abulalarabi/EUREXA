EUREXA

End-User Reconfiguration of Environment with eXplainable Augmentation for Generative Fabrication

Abstract

EUREXA presents a novel approach that enables end-users to reconfigure their environment through explainable augmentation techniques designed for generative fabrication. Our system combines user-friendly interfaces with powerful AI-driven tools to make digital fabrication more accessible and understandable to a broader audience.
